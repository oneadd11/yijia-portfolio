# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Reliability, failure-analysis, validation, product-quality, and engineering hiring managers reviewing the portfolio on laptop and mobile. They need to understand Jia's engineering judgment, scope of ownership, and ability to connect technical evidence with practical decisions.

## Product Purpose

Present a concise, public-safe engineering portfolio that demonstrates how Jia investigates failures, validates reliability, coordinates execution, and turns evidence into next-step decisions.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and evidence-led. The experience should feel confident and technically credible without becoming decorative, theatrical, or tool-focused.

## Anti-references

- Dense dashboard layouts that fragment one case into many equally weighted panels.
- Oversized hero typography that delays access to the case substance.
- Tiny body copy, low-contrast metadata, and compressed one-viewport storytelling.
- Generic SaaS card grids, decorative engineering imagery, and unverified claims.
- Tool inventories presented without explaining why the evidence led to the next action.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the engineering question and the decision, not the tools.
- Make the evidence-to-action sequence visible at a glance.
- Use restrained hierarchy so outcomes are prominent but not promotional.
- Preserve public-safe wording and distinguish observation from interpretation.
- Keep every case readable at normal laptop distance and structurally clear on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, skip links, semantic headings, and reduced-motion behavior. Body copy must remain comfortably readable, color cannot carry meaning alone, and layouts must avoid overflow from 320px upward.
