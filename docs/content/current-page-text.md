## Case 01: Multilayer Module Failure Localization & Root Cause Investigation

### Hero

**Case 01**

# Multilayer Module Failure Localization & Root Cause Investigation

Localized a sensing-voltage abnormality by correlating functional failure data with physical evidence and progressively testing competing failure hypotheses across a buried multilayer structure.

### Challenge

#### Pixel-Level Localization Through a Multilayer Stack

Sequential layer exposure maintained spatial registration with the failed-pixel location while preserving the surrounding evidence for subsequent analysis.

#### Failure-Relevant Feature Assessment Across Structural Variation

Spatial correlation with the failed-pixel location helped identify which particles, thickness variations, and interface features were relevant to the failure.

### Strategy

#### 01 Functional-to-Physical Localization

Used failed-pixel distribution and module architecture to define the physical region of interest.

#### 02 Cross-Method Evidence Correlation

Spatially correlated LIT, CSAM, and CT findings with the failed-pixel distribution to identify evidence consistent with the electrical failure pattern.

#### 03 Site-Specific Chip Verification

Transferred failed-pixel coordinates to the physical sample and performed targeted FIB analysis to assess direct chip-level damage.

#### 04 Adjacent-Layer Investigation

Extended FIB analysis into the immediately adjacent region to evaluate nearby structural features after direct chip damage was not supported.

### My Contribution

#### Select Region

Initial Localization to the Sensing-Chip Region

Used failed-pixel distribution and module architecture to define the first-priority region of interest before destructive analysis.

#### Weigh Evidence

Spatial Correlation of Nondestructive Evidence

Compared LIT, CSAM, and CT findings with failed-pixel locations to determine which indications were spatially relevant and worth pursuing.

#### Refine Hypothesis

FIB Assessment at the Selected Pixel

Site-specific FIB analysis found no direct damage at the selected pixel, shifting the investigation away from intrinsic chip failure.

#### Redirect Analysis

Redirecting the Analysis to Adjacent Structural Layers

Used the negative FIB result to shift follow-up cross-sectional analysis toward chip-adjacent layers and evaluate alternative structural causes.

### Conclusion

#### Failure Region Narrowed

The investigation converged from the full multilayer stack to the sensing-chip region and adjacent structural layers.

#### Direct Chip Damage Deprioritized

FIB analysis found no obvious chip damage or foreign-material intrusion, shifting attention away from intrinsic chip failure.

#### Structural Improvement Direction Defined

Prioritized layer geometry, thickness uniformity, and particle control near the affected region for follow-up validation and design improvement.

### Hero

**Case 02**

# Cross-functional Coordination for NAND Test Equipment Deployment

The project focused on bringing a custom NAND test system from initial setup through calibration, verification, and stable routine operation with local vendor support and remote NAND engineering guidance.

### Outcome

#### Core Functions Verified

Critical system functions validated for reliable test operation.

#### Stable Operation in ~3 Months

Stable operation achieved within the planned three-month window.

#### Deployment Readiness Achieved

Repeatable routine runs established before scheduled shipment.

### Challenge

- **Custom test equipment** required equipment-specific commissioning and repeated adjustment during early-stage operation.
- Troubleshooting depended on **multiple engineering teams** and coordinated input from the local equipment vendor.
- **On-site troubleshooting relied on timely remote engineering guidance** throughout commissioning and issue resolution.

### Strategy

- **Align** — establish shared requirements, responsibilities, and a clear view of current system status.
- **Track** — maintain visibility of alarms, configuration issues, pending actions, and technical ownership.
- **Stabilize** — move from commissioning and troubleshooting toward repeatable and reliable routine operation.

### My Contribution

1. **Tracked early setup issues through structured follow-up.** Recorded recurring calibration alarms, equipment communication issues, and abnormal system behavior; resolved operator-recoverable problems on site and escalated unresolved items with clear observations and status updates.
2. **Connected on-site execution with remote NAND guidance.** Coordinated day-to-day troubleshooting with the local vendor while sharing system status, alarm details, and test observations with the overseas NAND project lead.
3. **Supported equipment bring-up and stabilization.** Participated in calibration checks, configuration updates, and parameter adjustments as the equipment progressed from initial setup to stable operation.
4. **Handled routine NAND test operation after stabilization.** Performed chip loading, test recipe and parameter setup, routine test runs, and data collection, then reviewed results with the engineering team.
5. **Tracked verification through closure.** Followed remaining setup and verification items through closure and supported readiness confirmation before overseas shipment.

## Case 03: MLCC Design Engineering & Reliability Validation

### Hero

**Case 03**

# MLCC Design Engineering & Reliability Validation

### Section 01: Background

#### From Target Specifications to Qualification-Ready Designs

New product series and capacitance extensions for existing series required new MLCC designs to meet defined electrical targets while remaining structurally robust and manufacturable. Design options were evaluated through material selection, internal structure, electrical performance, and reliability testing before qualification.

#### My Task — Define and Validate Design Candidates

Developed internal structure options for different capacitance targets, evaluated their electrical and structural performance, and selected suitable candidates for reliability qualification and production validation.

#### Challenge — Balance Capacitance, Robustness, and Manufacturability

Each design had to meet its capacitance target without sacrificing structural robustness or manufacturing feasibility. The challenge was to define a practical design window before moving into qualification.

### Section 02: Design Window Development

#### Practical Design Range

Evaluated electrical performance together with structural and process constraints to define feasible design boundaries.

#### Design Margin Assessment

Compared trade-offs within the design range and retained candidates with enough structural and process margin at the target electrical performance.

#### Validation Candidate Selection

Selected candidates that met electrical targets and remained within existing process capability.

#### Key Design Variables

- Ceramic powder
- Dielectric thickness
- Layer count
- Electrode configuration
- Process constraints

### Section 03: Reliability Judgment

#### 01 Reliability Response Assessment

Reviewed reliability-test results and post-stress electrical behavior to identify abnormal responses and signs of possible structural weakness.

**Validation inputs:** TC / HALT, Cap / DF / IR / BV, Internal Criteria

#### 02 Qualification Judgment

Applied established acceptance criteria to determine qualification status and distinguish normal variation from responses that required further review.

#### 03 Engineering Disposition

Determined whether each design could proceed, required further review, or should move to failure investigation based on the combined test results.

**Decision label:** Proceed · Review · Investigate

### Section 04: Failure Analysis & Feedback

#### Electrical Response → Physical Evidence → Root Cause

Used physical characterization and failure analysis to investigate abnormal electrical behavior and identify the root cause. Correlated electrical results with physical evidence to determine the relevant failure features and identify the design or process variables requiring review before the next build and revalidation.

### Section 05: Design Closure

#### Revalidation

Revised designs were revalidated against the same electrical and reliability criteria before release.

#### Burn-In Screening

Established model-specific burn-in conditions, evaluated screening results, and used the findings to support release decisions.

#### Outcome

**3 products** advanced to mass production

**10+ models** supported from design development through qualification
